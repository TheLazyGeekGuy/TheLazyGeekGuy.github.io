## Why “Lazy”?

> Well-understood laziness is respecting your own time.

I started with the computing of the early days and went through legacy, cloud, CI/CD and now AI. My principle hasn’t changed: **simple, verifiable, automated**. The MIDI Studio editors illustrate it: every write to the hardware is read back and confirmed, nothing is stored without an explicit action, and known limits are documented rather than hidden.

## “Do you use AI?”

> — *Do you use AI?*
>
> *A question I was once asked, with a very serious face.*

Excellent question. Let us examine the exhibits: repositories, commits, tests, changelogs of suspicious polish. None of it constitutes a formal confession. Vibe-coding tools do not issue a signed receipt, and to my knowledge “wrote code very fast” has never been admitted as evidence in any court.

No confession, therefore **presumption of innocence**. And if a detector claims otherwise, remember that it also flags correct punctuation and bullet lists.
