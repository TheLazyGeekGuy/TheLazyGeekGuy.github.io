## Three areas, one common thread {#projects}

An employment contract, a building maintenance visit, a health document: different subjects, with the same need to find information, its context and its history. **Personal Life Management** brings together the workshop’s projects for these everyday records.

Each application keeps its own scope and development pace. **Domolyma already has a public demo. Prolyma and Sanolyma are preparing their public presentation.**

<ProjectGrid family="Productivité" />

## Prolyma · Working life {#prolyma}

**Your career, in your hands.**

Prolyma takes the individual worker’s perspective: follow your career and keep your own information across employers.

- **Professional records**: employers, employment periods, contracts, compensation and supporting documents.
- **Day-to-day work**: activity, time, expenses and their associated documents.
- **Career history**: skills, learning, moves and information to help prepare your next steps.

The project is in development, with no public demo announced at this stage. Its next presentation milestone is a discovery journey using fictional examples.

## Domolyma · Your properties’ life records {#domolyma}

**Find the information about a residence, then follow what needs doing.**

Domolyma brings together condominium and unit information, with views for property managers, owners’ councils, owners, residents and contractors.

- **Properties and documents**: residence, units, document vault, classification, search and versions.
- **Building life**: incident reports, equipment, contracts and maintenance visits.
- **Follow-up**: meeting preparation, decision records, actions and indicative budgets. A rental workspace complements landlords’ records.

The demo is a **public alpha in French and English**, using fictional data only. Changes stay in your browser, and the reset button restores the examples.

[Visit the website](https://thelazygeekguy.github.io/domolyma/) · [Try the demo](https://thelazygeekguy.github.io/domolyma/demo/) · [Browse the code](https://github.com/TheLazyGeekGuy/domolyma)

Further work includes meetings and voting, formal funding calls and complete accounting. Budgets and simulations in this alpha remain indicative.

## Sanolyma · Personal health history {#sanolyma}

**Keep a readable history, alongside its original documents.**

Sanolyma is an individual health-record project. Its direction is to find information over time and prepare useful documents for an appointment.

- **Original documents**: keep and find reports, prescriptions and other items in your own record.
- **A timeline**: connect documents, care and dated observations.
- **Preparation**: put together a personal selection and summary to review before sharing.

A public presentation is planned. Published examples will be fictional; personal records will remain private. The project focuses on organising a personal record, without automated diagnosis.
